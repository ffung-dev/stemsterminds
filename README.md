# STEMsterMinds Website

The official website for **STEMsterMinds**, a youth-led nonprofit organization dedicated to making Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM) education accessible, engaging, and inspiring for students around the world.

This website serves as the organization's central hub for sharing its mission, promoting events, publishing educational content, recruiting volunteers, and connecting with students, educators, and supporters. Built with scalability in mind, the website uses **Sanity CMS** so that nearly all content can be managed without modifying the codebase.

---

# About STEMsterMinds

STEMsterMinds is a youth-led nonprofit organization based in Canada and the United States that empowers young people to explore STEM through workshops, mentorship, educational resources, and community outreach. Our goal is to create opportunities that encourage curiosity, innovation, collaboration, and lifelong learning while making STEM more accessible to students of all backgrounds.

# Purpose

The STEMsterMinds website is designed to serve as the organization's public presence while making it easy for volunteers and administrators to manage content. By combining a modern frontend with a flexible content management system, the website can continue to grow alongside the organization while allowing nearly all content to be updated directly through Sanity CMS.

This is a [Next.js](https://nextjs.org) project bootstrapped with [`create-next-app`](https://nextjs.org/docs/app/api-reference/cli/create-next-app).
